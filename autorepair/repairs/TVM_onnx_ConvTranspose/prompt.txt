LLM attribution (onnx.ConvTranspose, [Bug], A=ONNX frontend):
Core defect: with a 5D input (3D transposed convolution), _impl_v1 directly raises
NotImplementedError "Relax ConvTranspose3d not supported yet", so legal ONNX models
cannot be imported.
Evidence: tvm.topi.nn.conv3d_transpose exists while relax.op.nn.conv3d_transpose is
missing, which shows the low-level support is available and the frontend imposes the
restriction artificially. The 1D/2D control group converts successfully and matches
onnxruntime differentially, proving the restriction is specific to 3D, not a false positive.
